**PLA base (real rig):** slides lie freely in the 300 mm frame, so the field is 310 mm whatever the slide count. Current code (6.5 mm datamatrix 18): 1080p 2.2, 8MP 3.8, 12MP 4.7, 16MP 5.4 ppm; width needed for 3 ppm: 2,575 px, 4 ppm: 3,434 px.

Caveats: ideal geometric ppm. Real effective resolution is lower (Bayer demosaicing, lens MTF, JPEG, focus), so treat ~3 ppm as a floor, not a target. Tilt/perspective shrinks ppm on the far side. Very high ppm with few slides needs a short working distance; check the lens minimum focus distance.
